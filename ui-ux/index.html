Cycle Quest
I designed a biking app for Ames that prioritizes safety and enjoyment, offering features like navigation, route discovery, speed tracking, bike shop locator, parking finder, road updates, and cyclist connections. Every step of the UI/UX design process, including user testing, shaped a practical and engaging user experience.

Dr. Pharm Online Shop Website
For this cosmetic online shop, I designed an aesthetically pleasing and user-friendly interface that accentuates the beauty and softness of the products being offered. The color palette revolves around pastel tones, conveying a sense of elegance and calmness that aligns with the brand’s focus on natural beauty. The design choices ensure an intuitive shopping experience, allowing users to explore product categories seamlessly while maintaining a visually harmonious flow throughout the website.

It is a website for "Sorush Hearing Clinic" which sells hearing aids and provides medical care for hearing matters. The design uses the exact shade of green they use for their interior design of the clinic, and orange is from their logo. I try simple and minimal designs for better accessibility.

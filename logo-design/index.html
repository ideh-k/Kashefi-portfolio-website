Logo Design

Santitas tortilla chips
For this project, I decided to take a crack at redesigning the Santitas logo. The current one is packed with details, but I wanted to explore something more simplified and bold. My proposed version pulls inspiration from three core elements: the sombrero and the tortilla chip, and smile! symbols that are instantly recognizable and connected to the brand’s roots.
The goal was to keep the character and authenticity while making it cleaner, more modern, and versatile for packaging and digital use.
Logo Design for Prtian Jewelry
This logo design for Partian Jewelry Shop is inspired by the rich heritage of Parthian soldiers in Iranian history, as seen in ancient artworks. The bold and minimal geometric forms draw directly from the soldier's silhouette, blending their strength and historical significance with the refined delicacy of jewelry craftsmanship. The color palette features charcoal and gold, carefully chosen to add depth and meaning to the design. Charcoal symbolizes resilience and pays homage to the stone carvings of Parthian soldiers, while gold represents the elegance and opulence of jewelry. This combination creates a timeless and modern symbol that reflects both the enduring legacy of Iranian culture and the sophistication of luxury branding. The design captures the spirit of resilience and beauty, aligning perfectly with the shop's vision.
Logo Design for ع Cafe
This logo design incorporates the Persian letter "ع" from the café's name, seamlessly blending it with the silhouette of a coffee cup to represent the coffee shop’s identity. While the concept may seem classic, the client requested a simple and straightforward design that feels relatable and welcoming. I kept the design minimal and approachable to resonate with the café’s target audience.
The color palette features yellow and green, each chosen to reflect the café's atmosphere. Yellow represents energy and brightness, symbolizing the lively and vibrant experience of the café's customers. Conversely, green conveys calmness and coziness, creating a sense of safety and focus for visitors.

Interior Design - 3D

I specialize in creating detailed and realistic 3D models of interior spaces and objects using Blender software. My designs focus on both aesthetics and functionality, allowing clients to visualize and explore various design concepts in a 3D environment.

Cafe Diem Re-Branding Project
Revitalizing a downtown Ames coffee shop through a rebranding initiative. The primary objective is to cultivate a distinctive identity characterized by a niche, minimalistic, and clean aesthetic. The creative direction is inspired by a space and cosmos theme, aligning seamlessly with the project brief to create a visually engaging and unique brand and interior presence. For this project, the actual coffee shop's map was used to design the interior accurately.
Room Interior Design
This 3D project involved rendering an isometric view of a bedroom, with the primary focus on exploring the simulation engines and the intricate application of textures. The bedroom was meticulously modeled to capture a cozy and modern aesthetic, featuring detailed furniture, soft lighting, and subtle decorative elements. The project aimed to push the boundaries of texture realism, from the soft fabric of the bedding to the wood grain of the flooring and furniture. Using simulation engines allowed for a more dynamic environment, enabling real-time adjustments and enhancing the overall quality of the render.

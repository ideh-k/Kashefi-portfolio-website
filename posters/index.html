Posters

Here is my collection of posters, where creativity meets design. Each piece showcases bold visuals, innovative layouts, and attention to detail, reflecting a unique blend of art and storytelling.

2020 Olympic poster
The main elements in this design were the 2020 Olympics taking place in Japan and using the color pallet of the Japan flag and cherry blossoms as another symbol of Japan.
Blue ray glasses Advertisement
This advertising poster design focuses on the benefits of Blue Ray (or blue-cut) glasses, highlighting their protective qualities against harmful blue light emitted by digital screens.
Cafe decoration poster
These posters are designed for a cozy and friendly interior setting of a Coffee shop, using a chalkboard-style canvas to create a warm, welcoming atmosphere. The designs incorporate charming bakery elements paired with scattered flour creatively swirling through the composition like a galaxy, evoking a "space theme" with a culinary twist. The blend of the black chalkboard backdrop with white chalk-like drawings of flour and bakery elements adds a rustic, homey feel.
Psychologist Day
This minimalist black-and-white poster celebrates Psychologist Day with clean typography and symbolic design elements. The brain and heart icons in the word "Psychologist" represent the importance of both intellect and compassion in the field of psychology.
Speed limit Poster
This impactful poster highlights the dangers of driving too slowly on highways. The image of a turtle in the middle of the road, surrounded by fast-moving cars, serves as a visual metaphor, emphasizing that driving at an excessively slow speed can be just as hazardous as speeding. The message encourages drivers to maintain a safe and appropriate speed for the road conditions.
Photography workshop Poster
This design uses vector art and gradients to digitally illustrate a camera, capturing the essence of the workshop theme. Created in Illustrator, the poster combines sleek lines and subtle color transitions to draw attention while maintaining a clean, modern aesthetic that effectively communicates the event's focus on photography.
